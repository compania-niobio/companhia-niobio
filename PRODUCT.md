## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step, deployed on Vercel (confirmed in the brief).

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium Brazilian businesses who either have no digital presence yet or have one that is scattered and does not bring clients. They visit to understand what Niobio does, who does the work, what it costs, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Niobio is a technology and digital marketing company: website creation, SEO and artificial intelligence. The institutional site explains the offer, introduces the founder, shows real projects, lists the SEO subscription plans and leads to a first conversation (WhatsApp, e-mail, Instagram). Success means a visitor reaches a contact channel with a clear idea of the next step.
